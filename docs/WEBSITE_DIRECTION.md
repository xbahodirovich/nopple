# Nopple website and Editor

## Current direction, 11 October 2026
The user’s video reference supersedes the previous black Air palette: preserve oversized typography and layered scroll movement, but make the website warm and cozy. Use warm ivory (#f5eee4), walnut (#392b24), clay (#995b40), and sand (#e4d3bb). Retain the supplied turquoise nopple-icon.png unchanged as the brand mark and favicon.

Homepage is an invitation, not an editable residence. Sunlit architectural inspiration leads into /editor. The photograph grows and tilts gently into view while its image moves at another rate; large text and the decorative logo move at separate scroll rates. Lower sections reveal once with a short stagger. No scroll interception or forced loading intro. Reduced motion leaves all content visible with stationary imagery.

Controls use consistent rounded corners, clear contrast and native keyboard interaction. The language picker uses a styled native base-select where supported and the platform picker elsewhere. English, Russian and Uzbek remain reversible and persistent. The Editor uses warm surfaces without changing model materials. AI remains disconnected.

Verification: scripts/check-warm.mjs checks homepage widths 320/390/768/1024/1440/1920 and Editor widths 320/390/768/1024/1440 across all three languages, scroll movement, reduced motion and logo loading. Screenshots live in .impeccable/review/warm. Browser emulation does not replace physical-device testing.
